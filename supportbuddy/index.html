// Autism Companion

SupportBuddy

A calm, personalised AI companion designed for people with autism spectrum disorder. Chat by voice or text — in your own language, at your own pace.

🍎 App Store ▶ Google Play

// What SupportBuddy offers

🎤

Voice & Text Chat

Speak or type — SupportBuddy listens and replies out loud, in a voice chosen just for you.

🌍

8 Languages

Responds as a native speaker in English, Greek, French, German, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, and Swedish.

🧠

Personalised Profile

Remembers your sensory sensitivities, interests, and calming preferences to give the right kind of support.

😌

Calming Support

Detects stress in conversation and gently guides you through breathing, counting, or quiet rest.

🔒

Private by Design

Your profile and conversation history are stored on your device only — never uploaded to our servers.

👨‍👩‍👧

All Ages

Voice profiles and language complexity adapt for children, teens, and adults. Parents can assist with setup.

Need help?

If you have a question about SupportBuddy, found a bug, or want to request a feature, we're here to help. We typically respond within 24 hours.
