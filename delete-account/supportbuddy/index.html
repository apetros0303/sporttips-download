// Account

Delete My Account

You can request deletion of your SupportBuddy account and all associated personal data at any time. We process all requests within 30 days.

// Option 1 — In the app

Delete from the app

1
Open SupportBuddy Launch the app on your device.
2
Go to Profile Tap the Profile card on the Home screen.
3
Tap "Delete Account" Scroll to the bottom of the Profile screen and tap Delete Account. Confirm when prompted — this action is permanent.

// Option 2 — Email request

Send us a deletion request

Email us from the address linked to your account with the subject line "Delete Account".

petrosalex66@gmail.com
We'll confirm your deletion via email within 30 days. If you no longer have access to your registered email, include your in-app display name so we can locate your account.
